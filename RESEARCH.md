## Positioning

**Primary:** A neighborhood barbershop in Spring City that brings traditional barbering and real community back to Main Street — clean cuts, sharp beard work, and a place where you’re welcome even if you’re not getting a haircut.

**Supporting:** Precision work done right every time, at prices that make sense for local families, owned by a local barber with 20+ years in the chair.

## Competitors (brief)

1. Kathi’s Barber Shop (Spring City) — long-standing, walk-in friendly, affordable; gap: less modern fade/precision focus and community storytelling.
2. New Street Barber Shop (Spring City) — local classic; gap: newer shop with stronger online presence and specific service menu.
3. Iron’s Edge (Phoenixville, previous shop of owner) — established reputation; gap: Neighbors offers the local Main Street hangout feel.
4. Sport Clips / chain options nearby — convenience; gap: independent, personal, traditional razor and community focus.
5. Revival Barber Co. (nearby) — modern/creative; gap: more classic neighborhood hangout positioning.

Strength to match: Consistent quality and local loyalty. Gap to fill: Clear online booking, full service transparency, and gallery of real work.
