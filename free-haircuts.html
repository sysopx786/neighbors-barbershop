Community Special · Spring City, PA

Free Back-to-School Haircuts

Starting Tuesday, August 18, the amazing folks at Neighbors Barbershop are giving FREE haircuts to school-age kids thanks to the generosity of our community.

Call (267) 566-7871 Get Directions

How It Works

Just walk in, tell them you’re there for a free school haircut, and that’s it. No questions asked. No tip needed. Completely FREE!

Whether you’re a millionaire or struggling, any school-age child can come in and get a fresh cut. The haircut is paid for, including the tip. Spend that money on school supplies, new shoes, or groceries instead.

Who Qualifies

Any school-age child who needs a haircut. No income requirements. No questions asked.

When

Starting Tuesday, August 18. Call the shop for current availability and hours.

Where

105 N. Main Street, Spring City, PA. Walk-ins welcome for this special.

Thank You to the Community

A huge thank you to Justin and all the barbers at Neighbors Barbershop—and to YOU, our community, for once again stepping up and making something great happen for our kids!

This is what community is all about.
