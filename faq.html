FAQ

Common questions. Call or book if you need something specific.

What’s a Men’s Traditional cut?

Clean lines, shorter sides, longer top. Gradual blend. Not a skin fade. About 30 minutes, $35.

How’s a skin fade different?

It blends all the way to the skin. Tell us low, mid, or high. Most guys rebook every 2–3 weeks. $40 · 30 min.

Do you cut kids’ hair?

Yes. Ages 12 and under, any style, $30. Parents can stay. We go at their pace. Kids can stop in for a water even when they’re not getting cut.

What’s included in Traditional & Beard or Skin Fade & Beard?

The haircut plus full beard shape and hot towel. Traditional & Beard $40 / 45 min. Skin Fade & Beard $45 / 45 min.

Do you do straight-razor head shaves?

Yes. Hot towel, hot lather with oils, razor, second towel, and moisturizing scrub. $30 for head, $50 for head & beard.

What is The Neighborhood Standard?

Everything: any haircut, beard with hot towel, moisturizer, full waxing (ears/nose/brows), and cold press. $75 · about an hour.

Walk-ins or appointments only?

Appointments preferred and easy to book online. Walk-ins welcome when we have space. Call to check.

How do I book?

Book online here or call (267) 566-7871.

Where are you and what are the hours?

105 N. Main Street, Spring City, PA. Tuesday–Saturday 9 AM–6 PM. Closed Monday and Sunday.
