Mayor Adam Alberico, free school haircuts, and the neighbors who made it happen.

Mayor Adam Alberico and Justin Kyle talking about free school haircuts.

Mayor Adam Alberico at Neighbors

The mayor stopped by to talk with Justin about free haircuts for school-age kids. Clients donated so any child could walk in, say they were there for a school cut, and leave with a fresh look — tip included.

Rocco’s first beard trim

The mayor also got a cut and beard trim from Rocco, an apprentice at the shop. It was Rocco’s first beard trim. The mayor said he did a great job.

“Got a fresh cut today from Rocco… he did a great job. Keep up the great work, Rocco!”

— Mayor Adam Alberico

Free haircuts for school-age kids

Thanks to community donations, school-age kids can walk in for a free haircut. No questions asked. No tip needed. The cut and tip are covered so families can spend that money on school supplies, shoes, or groceries.

Call the shop or stop by 105 N. Main Street to ask about current availability.
