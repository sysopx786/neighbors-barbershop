Mayor Adam Alberico, school haircuts, and the neighbors who paid for them.

Mayor Adam Alberico and Justin Kyle talking about school haircuts.

Mayor Adam Alberico at Neighbors

The mayor stopped by to talk with Justin about haircuts for school-age kids. Clients had left money so a kid could walk in, say they were there for a school cut, and leave with it done. The tip was covered too.

Rocco’s first beard trim

The mayor also sat for a cut and a beard trim from Rocco, an apprentice. That trim was Rocco’s first. The mayor said he did a great job.

“He did a great job. Keep up the great work, Rocco.”

Mayor Adam Alberico

Haircuts for school-age kids

The mayor announced the donated cuts for the week starting Tuesday, August 18, 2026. A kid could walk in, say they were there for a school cut, and that was it. The point was to spend that money on school supplies, shoes, or groceries.
