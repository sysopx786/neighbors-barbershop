What We Collect

This website itself collects minimal personal information:

  • No user accounts or direct form submissions on this site.
  • Appointment booking is handled entirely by Square Appointments. Their privacy policy applies to data you enter there (name, phone, email, appointment details).
  • Embedded Google Maps on the contact page may set cookies or collect location-related data under Google’s policies.
  • Google Fonts may be loaded (third-party request).
  • Standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited) may be kept temporarily by the hosting provider (GitHub Pages).

We do not sell personal information. We do not collect unnecessary data.

How We Use Information

Booking data is used by Square to schedule and manage appointments. We use contact details you provide to respond to inquiries or appointment-related questions. Analytics or advertising cookies are not currently used on this site.

Third Parties

Square Appointments processes booking data. Google provides maps and fonts. Social media links go to Instagram and Facebook. Review these services’ own privacy policies for details.

Children

Services for children (kid’s haircuts) require a parent or guardian to book or accompany the child. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. For kids’ cuts, parental involvement is expected.

Your Rights & Data Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold (or that Square holds related to your bookings). Email theneighborsbarber@gmail.com or call (267) 566-7871. We will respond within a reasonable time. For Square data, contact them directly or through their account tools.

Cookies

See our Cookie Policy. Currently, this site does not set non-essential cookies itself. Third-party services may.

Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect information. Booking data is retained by Square according to their policies. Contact us with any security concerns.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date will change. Continued use of the site means you accept the updated policy.
