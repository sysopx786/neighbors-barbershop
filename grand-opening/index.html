A new barbershop. A neighborhood celebration.

On Saturday, June 13, 2026, Neighbors Barbershop celebrated its grand opening at 105 N. Main Street in Spring City, Pennsylvania. Friends, families, and neighbors came together to welcome a new place for fresh cuts, conversation, and community.

✦ Saturday, June 13, 2026⌖ 105 N. Main Street · Spring City, PA◷ Festivities began around 11 AM

The invitation

It started with an invitation to Main Street.

The original grand-opening poster invited everyone to stop in, meet the barbers, spend time together, and celebrate a new business on Main Street.

The event was announced for Saturday, June 13, beginning around 11 AM, with free hot dogs and hamburgers for visitors.
